# ADR 0034 — Model-grounded engineering and evaluation

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-11-21  
Supersedes: —  
Superseded by: —  

Origin: Consolidates decisions taken between 2026-05 and 2026-11, before publication.

## Context

Behaviour generated by a hosted model at inference time changes application engineering: evaluation-driven gates
replace pass/fail tests, instructions and context configurations become versioned artifacts, guardrails cover tool
use and privilege. `CAP-ITO-EXP-8` covered a model helping a developer, not a team building a model-grounded feature.

## Decision

1. `CAP-ADM-ENG-6` Model-Grounded Behaviour Engineering and `CAP-ADM-ENG-7` Autonomous-Behaviour Guardrail Design
   join ADM-ENG (ADR 0021), not a new group: building generated behaviour is application engineering, as evaluating
   it is application testing. `CAP-ADM-ENG-6` consumes the model-provider gateway (`CAP-ITO-SUB-8`); `CAP-ADM-ENG-7`
   `depends_on` it and takes governed semantic constraints (`CAP-BIA-GOV-7`, ADR 0031) as one rule source.
2. `CAP-ADM-QAT-6` Generative & Trajectory Evaluation and `CAP-ADM-QAT-7` AI Red-Teaming & Adversarial Testing are
   siblings in ADM-QAT, both `depends_on` `CAP-ADM-QAT-1`: no single ground truth, a scorer whose
   agreement with experts must be measured, the path taken as a target; evaluation is offline and sampled, guardrails
   online.
3. ADM-ENG functions: `FUNC-adm-context-assembly-design`, `FUNC-adm-model-grounded-component-design` (also
   `CAP-ADM-ENG-1`), `FUNC-adm-model-selection-policy`, `FUNC-adm-inference-artifact-versioning` (also
   `CAP-ADM-ENG-2`), `FUNC-adm-guardrail-design`. ADM-QAT functions: `FUNC-adm-eval-set-curation`,
   `FUNC-adm-deterministic-assertion-scoring`, `FUNC-adm-model-graded-scoring`, `FUNC-adm-judge-calibration`,
   `FUNC-adm-trajectory-scoring`, `FUNC-adm-regression-evaluation` (also `CAP-ADM-QAT-5`),
   `FUNC-adm-production-evaluation-sampling`, `FUNC-adm-guardrail-effectiveness-evaluation` (also `CAP-ADM-ENG-7`),
   and `FUNC-adm-adversarial-testing` on `CAP-ADM-QAT-7`.
4. `FUNC-adm-context-assembly-design` specifies at design time what a component may see; the runtime
   `FUNC-ito-context-assembly` (ADR 0033) is a separate function. `FUNC-adm-guardrail-effectiveness-evaluation` evaluates
   the guardrail, not performs it. `FUNC-adm-model-graded-scoring` states in its definition that a score is evidence,
   not ground truth, meaningful only with its calibration (`FUNC-adm-judge-calibration`).
5. Two human personas, defined by responsibility, not job title (ADR 0012): `PERSONA-adm-model-grounded-engineer`
   and `PERSONA-adm-ai-assurance`, the latter split from `PERSONA-adm-app-assurance` on competence rather than
   responsibility and marked a consolidation candidate; the non-human actor stays `AUTON-ACTOR` (ADR 0030).
6. Amendments: `CAP-ADM-ENG-2`'s record includes the non-code artifacts shaping inference-time behaviour, versioned
   with source; `CAP-ADM-ENG-3` admits a delegated actor as an interface consumer, with "agent experience" and "AX"
   as aliases, not terms (ADR 0007); `CAP-ADM-QAT-1`'s criterion is statistical where behaviour is generated;
   `CAP-ADM-QAT-5` carries regression evaluation.

## Consequences

- Coding assistance and model-grounded product engineering are distinguishable; neither pretends to be the other.
- ADM-ENG and ADM-QAT read less tightly conventional, and `PERSONA-adm-ai-assurance` is borderline; both recorded,
  not defended.
- Capability names are cheap to rename, function ids are not; `FUNC-adm-model-graded-scoring` is likeliest to age.

## Alternatives

- A sibling group for agentic engineering — building generated behaviour would be a separate discipline while
  evaluating it stays testing.
- Evaluation as a level of functional testing (`CAP-ADM-QAT-2`) — no ground truth and no trajectory there.
- Fold adversarial testing into evaluation — couples practices with different cadence and tooling.
- "Agent experience" as a term — single-analyst-sourced; an alias can be promoted.
